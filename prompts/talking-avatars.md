# Talking avatar prompts

Presenter-led videos: a person (real with consent, or fully synthetic) speaking a script to camera. This is a different job from cinematic generation — what matters is the script, the voice, the delivery and a setting that does not distract. For the [AI avatar generator](https://vivideo.ai/features/ai-avatar-generator) and spokesperson formats.

There are two ways to make a talking video:

1. **Avatar + voice** — choose or create an avatar, pick a voice from the [AI voice generator](https://vivideo.ai/features/ai-voice-generator), paste the script. Best for anything longer than a few sentences and for a presenter who must look the same in every video.
2. **Video model with native speech** — write the line into a prompt for a model that generates audio. Best for short, cinematic spoken moments (see [which models have native audio](https://vivideo.ai/research/which-ai-video-models-have-native-audio)).

Tips for this category:

- Write for the ear: short sentences, contractions, one idea per sentence. Read it aloud before generating.
- Use punctuation to direct delivery: commas for short pauses, a full stop for a beat, an em dash for a turn.
- Keep the background calm and slightly out of focus; let gesture and expression carry the shot.
- Only create an avatar or clone a voice of someone who has given explicit consent.

## Avatar scripts (paste into an avatar video)

### 1. Product walkthrough opener

```
Setting: modern home office, soft daylight, plant and bookshelf blurred behind. Framing: medium close-up, eye level. Tone: warm, confident, conversational.
Script: "Hi, I'm Sam. In the next sixty seconds I'll show you how to set up your first project — no manual, no guesswork. Let's start with the dashboard."
16:9.
```

### 2. Course module intro

```
Setting: bright studio with a soft grey backdrop. Framing: medium shot, presenter slightly off-center to leave room for on-screen text. Tone: clear, encouraging, measured pace.
Script: "Welcome to module three. So far you've learned the basics. Today we put them together — and by the end, you'll build something real."
16:9.
```

### 3. Internal announcement

```
Setting: company office lounge, natural light, slightly blurred colleagues in the background. Framing: medium close-up. Tone: friendly, direct, upbeat.
Script: "Quick update for everyone. Starting Monday, the new booking system is live. It takes two minutes to learn, and there's a guide linked below."
16:9.
```

### 4. Short social explainer

```
Setting: clean kitchen counter, morning light. Framing: vertical, head and shoulders, centered. Tone: energetic, punchy, creator-style.
Script: "Most people store coffee in the fridge. Don't. Here's what actually keeps it fresh — and it's cheaper."
9:16.
```

### 5. Customer support answer

```
Setting: calm neutral office with soft lighting. Framing: medium close-up. Tone: patient, reassuring, slow.
Script: "If your order hasn't arrived yet, don't worry. First, check the tracking link in your confirmation email. If it hasn't moved in three days, reply to that email and we'll sort it out."
16:9.
```

### 6. Multilingual welcome (dub after generating)

```
Setting: hotel lobby with warm lighting. Framing: medium shot. Tone: hospitable, smiling, relaxed.
Script: "Welcome! We're so glad you're here. Breakfast is served from seven to ten on the terrace, and our team is available all day if you need anything."
16:9. After rendering, dub into the guest languages you need with lip sync.
```

## Native-speech video prompts

### 7. Spokesperson in the field

```
A woman in her forties in a navy field jacket stands on a windy coastal path, hair moving, looks into the camera and says: "This coastline loses a meter a year. Here's what we're doing about it." Medium shot, handheld but steady, wind noise and distant waves. Overcast soft light, documentary realism. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Founder-to-camera

```
A man in his late twenties in a plain black t-shirt sits on a stool in a small workshop full of prototypes, leans forward slightly and says: "We built the first one in this room. It didn't work. The fortieth did." Medium close-up, slow push-in, 50mm lens. Warm tungsten practical lights, shallow depth of field. Honest documentary style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. News-style presenter

```
A presenter in her thirties in a tailored burgundy blazer stands in a bright modern studio with large screens behind her showing abstract blue graphics, and says clearly: "Good evening. Here are tonight's top stories." Medium shot, static camera, crisp even studio lighting. Professional broadcast look, no readable text on screens. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 10. Doctor explaining, calm

```
A doctor in her fifties in a white coat sits at a desk in a bright clinic office, hands folded, and says gently: "It's very common, and it's very treatable. Let me show you what happens next." Medium close-up, static camera at eye level. Soft daylight from a side window. Warm, reassuring tone. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 11. Two-person podcast moment

```
Two hosts sit across from each other at a wooden table with microphones in a cozy podcast studio with acoustic panels and warm lamps. The host on the left laughs and says: "No, but that's exactly the point." The other nods, smiling. Two-shot, slight slow dolly. Warm practical lighting, shallow depth of field. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 12. Animated mascot speaker

```
A cheerful cartoon owl wearing round glasses stands on a stack of books and says to the camera, wings gesturing: "Ready for today's word? Let's go!" Medium shot, static camera. Bright, soft 3D animation style, warm library background. Friendly children's learning channel tone. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```
