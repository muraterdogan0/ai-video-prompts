# Social shorts prompts (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

Vertical clips built to stop a scroll in the first second. The first frame is the hook: something already moving, a strange image, or a face mid-sentence. This pack is for [TikTok](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/tiktok-video-maker), [Instagram Reels](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/instagram-reels-maker) and [YouTube Shorts](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/youtube-shorts-maker).

Tips for this category:

- Start the action at frame one. Write "the clip opens mid-action" or "already in motion" — avoid slow fade-ins.
- 9:16 is the default. Keep faces and key objects in the middle 60% of the frame so captions and platform UI do not cover them.
- Loops perform well: describe the last moment matching the first (see prompt 6).
- Plan the hook before the prompt; [how to hook viewers in short-form video](https://vivideo.ai/blog/how-to-hook-viewers-short-form-video) has patterns that work.
- Add captions afterwards with the [subtitle generator](https://vivideo.ai/tools/subtitle-generator); most short-form is watched on mute.

## Prompts

### 1. Impossible-scale hook

```
The clip opens already in motion: a tiny person the size of a coffee bean runs across a giant wooden kitchen table, dodging a rolling blueberry the size of a boulder. Low camera at table level tracks alongside the runner. Bright morning window light, shallow depth of field, photoreal miniature look. Playful, surprising. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 2. Satisfying cut

```
Close-up of a sharp knife slicing slowly through a glossy rainbow-layered jelly cake on a marble board, the layers wobbling and catching the light. Camera static, top-down 45-degree angle. Soft bright studio light, pastel background. Oddly satisfying ASMR aesthetic, crisp slicing sound. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 3. Talking-head hot take

```
A confident woman in her late twenties in a mustard sweater leans toward a phone camera in a bright apartment and says, without any pause before speaking: "Stop posting at 9 a.m. Here's why." She raises one eyebrow. Front-facing phone camera, ring light reflected in her eyes, slight handheld movement. Creator-style, punchy. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 4. Transformation reveal

```
A dull, empty grey bedroom transforms in one continuous shot: walls wash into warm sage green, a bed with linen sheets appears, plants grow up from the corners, string lights switch on. The camera slowly pushes in from the doorway during the transformation. Soft evening light by the end. Smooth magical makeover style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 5. POV hook

```
First-person POV walking into a tiny hidden bookshop through a narrow green door, the camera pushing past hanging plants into a warm room stacked floor to ceiling with books, a cat asleep on the counter. Steady gimbal movement, warm tungsten lamps, dust in the light. Cozy, discovery vibe. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 6. Seamless loop

```
A paper boat sails along a narrow stream through moss and pebbles, the camera tracking beside it at water level; the stream curves and the shot ends on exactly the same framing it started on, so the clip loops seamlessly. Soft forest light, gentle ripples, calm and hypnotic. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 7. Micro-tutorial step

```
Top-down shot of hands on a white desk folding a square of blue paper into an origami crane, sped up so all the folds happen in one smooth sequence, finishing with the crane lifted toward the camera. Bright even daylight, clean minimal background. Tutorial-friendly, clear hand movements. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Trend dance (fictional character)

```
A cheerful cartoon-style fox in a denim jacket performs a short, bouncy dance on a sunny rooftop, hips swaying, ending in a pose pointing at the camera. Static camera, full-body shot. Bright saturated 3D animation style, clean shadows, blue sky. Fun, family-friendly. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 9. Before / after swipe

```
A messy closet full of tangled clothes. A hand swipes across the screen from left to right and the shot transitions into the same closet perfectly organized with color-sorted hangers and labelled boxes. Locked-off camera, the swipe acts as a wipe transition. Bright neutral light. Satisfying home-organization content. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 10. Fitness quick tip

```
A fit woman in a black training set demonstrates a slow, controlled bodyweight squat in a sunlit home gym, side-on to the camera so her form is clear. Static full-body shot at hip height. Bright natural light, clean wooden floor, plants in the background. Instructional but energetic fitness creator style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 11. Day-in-the-life cut

```
A fast montage feel within one clip: a young baker dusts flour onto a wooden bench, shapes a loaf, slides it into a glowing oven, and pulls out a golden crusty loaf, each action quickly following the last. Handheld close-ups, warm early-morning bakery light, flour in the air. Vlog-style, energetic pace. 9:16, 10 seconds.
```

### 12. Curiosity science visual

```
Extreme close-up of a tall glass of sparkling water as a handful of raisins drop in; bubbles cling to them, they float up to the surface, release the bubbles and sink again in a slow rhythm. Camera static, side-on, shallow depth of field. Bright clean kitchen light against a white background. Curious, educational short-form look. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 13. Pinterest-style aesthetic

```
Slow, dreamy vertical shot of linen curtains billowing in a breeze beside a window, a ceramic vase of dried flowers on the sill, afternoon light casting soft shadows across a cream wall. Very slow push-in. Warm muted tones, film-like softness. Calm, aspirational home aesthetic. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```
