# Cinematic storytelling prompts

Shots for short films, trailers, music-video narratives and story-driven content. The craft here is continuity: making shot 2 look like it belongs in the same film as shot 1. For [AI movie generation](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-movie-generator) and narrative work.

Tips for this category:

- **Write a character sheet once and paste it into every shot.** "Mara, early thirties, short black bob, olive trench coat, silver ring on her left hand" — the same words, every time.
- **Lock the look.** Reuse one style line across the film: "anamorphic 35mm film, teal-and-amber grade, soft halation, fine grain".
- For strong continuity, generate a keyframe still of the character first (for example with [Nano Banana Pro](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/nano-banana-pro) or the [AI image generator](https://vivideo.ai/guides/ai-image-generator)) and animate it with image-to-video.
- Cover a scene like a film crew would: establishing wide, medium, close-up, insert, reaction. Then cut them together in the [AI video editor](https://vivideo.ai/features/ai-video-editor) or join them with the [video merger](https://vivideo.ai/tools/video-merger).
- [Manual mode](https://vivideo.ai/features/manual-mode) lets you pick the model and settings per shot.

## Single shots

### 1. Establishing wide: rain city

```
Wide establishing shot of a rain-soaked city street at night, neon signs in an unnamed script reflecting in puddles, a lone figure in an olive trench coat crossing under a streetlight. Slow crane down from rooftop height to street level. Anamorphic 35mm film look, teal-and-amber grade, soft halation around lights, fine grain. 2.39:1 framing within 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 2. Close-up emotion

```
Extreme close-up of a woman in her early thirties with a short black bob, rain on her face, as she reads a folded letter; her jaw tightens and her eyes fill but she does not cry. Very slow push-in, 85mm lens, background fully blurred. Single soft key light from a window, deep shadows. Anamorphic film look, fine grain. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 3. Desert chase

```
A battered red pickup truck races across a cracked desert salt flat, a towering dust cloud behind it, as a black motorcycle closes in from the left. Low tracking shot from a camera car running alongside, slight vibration. Harsh midday sun, bleached colors, heat shimmer on the horizon. Gritty action-film style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 4. Fantasy reveal

```
A young knight in worn leather armor pushes through tall reeds and stops at the edge of a misty lake, where an enormous stone statue of a kneeling giant rises from the water. The camera starts behind her shoulder and cranes up and over to reveal the statue. Overcast pearl-grey light, drifting fog, painterly epic fantasy realism. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 5. Sci-fi corridor

```
A lone astronaut in a white suit walks slowly down a long, curved spaceship corridor lit by flickering strip lights, some panels dark. Symmetrical steady tracking shot from behind. Cold blue-white light, haze in the air, metallic reflections. Tense, minimalist sci-fi film look. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 6. Period drama interior

```
In a candlelit 18th-century drawing room, an elderly man in a powdered wig sets down a wine glass and looks up slowly as the door opens off screen. Medium shot, static camera, slight push-in at the end. Warm candlelight as the only source, deep shadows, rich textures of velvet and wood. Painterly period-drama cinematography. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 7. Horror tension

```
A dim hallway in an old farmhouse at night; at the far end a bedroom door slowly creaks open by itself, revealing only darkness. Static wide shot, camera at doorknob height. Faint moonlight from a window, cold desaturated blue tones, heavy shadows, subtle film grain. Slow-burn horror atmosphere. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Noir dialogue

```
Black-and-white film noir: a detective in a fedora sits across a table from a nervous man in a smoky interrogation room and says quietly: "Start from the beginning." Over-the-shoulder shot on the nervous man, venetian-blind shadows striping the wall. Hard single key light, deep blacks, high contrast. 4:3 framing within 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. Animated feature look

```
A curious young girl with a red scarf and oversized boots peeks out from behind a mossy tree at a small glowing forest spirit shaped like a lantern. Medium shot, gentle push-in. Warm stylized 3D feature-animation look, soft volumetric light through the trees, rich saturated greens. Whimsical and heartfelt. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 10. Game cinematic

```
A massive armored war machine strides through a ruined city at dusk, crushing a car underfoot as sparks fly; a squad of soldiers takes cover behind rubble in the foreground. Low-angle tracking shot moving backward ahead of the machine. Orange firelight against a purple sky, smoke and embers, high-end game trailer cinematic style. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

## Multi-shot sequences

Some models accept several shots in one prompt. Where they don't, generate each shot separately with the same character and style lines.

### 11. Three-shot scene: the call

```
Shot 1: Wide shot of a small, cluttered apartment at night, a phone buzzing on the table, a man in his forties in a grey cardigan asleep on the couch.
Shot 2: Close-up of his hand reaching for the phone, the screen glow lighting his face as he squints.
Shot 3: Medium shot as he sits up suddenly, fully awake, and whispers: "Where are you?"
Consistent style across all shots: muted naturalistic color, warm practical lamp light, handheld intimate camera, 35mm film grain. 16:9, 10 seconds total.
```

### 12. Trailer beat sequence

```
Shot 1: Extreme wide of a lighthouse on a black rock in a storm, waves exploding against it.
Shot 2: Inside, a lighthouse keeper with a grey beard lights an oil lamp with shaking hands.
Shot 3: Close-up of the lamp's flame flaring bright as thunder cracks.
Same look throughout: cold blue night, warm orange lamplight, heavy rain, epic trailer cinematography, slow deliberate camera moves. 16:9, 12 seconds total.
```

### 13. Character continuity template

```
Character: Mara, early thirties, short black bob, olive trench coat, silver ring on left hand.
Look: anamorphic 35mm film, teal-and-amber grade, soft halation, fine grain.
Shot: [DESCRIBE THE ACTION AND SETTING]. Camera: [SHOT SIZE, MOVE, LENS]. Light: [SOURCE AND MOOD].
16:9, 8 seconds.
```
