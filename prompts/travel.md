# Travel prompts

Destination reels, hotel and resort promos, aerials, itinerary b-roll and "wish you were here" moments. For [AI video for travel](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-video-for-travel), tour operators, hotels and travel creators.

Tips for this category:

- **Don't misrepresent a real place.** Generated footage of a specific hotel, beach or landmark can look plausible and still be wrong. For a real property or destination, animate your own photos with [image to video](https://vivideo.ai/features/image-to-video), or describe a generic location ("a Mediterranean fishing village") rather than naming it.
- Travel lives on light: golden hour, blue hour, morning mist. Pick one per shot.
- Human scale sells the place: a small figure on a ridge, a hand on a railing, feet in the sand.
- Build reels from 6–8 shots of 3–5 seconds each, cut to music.

## Prompts

### 1. Cliffside village aerial

```
Smooth drone shot flying slowly toward a whitewashed cliffside village above a deep blue sea, terraces of houses with blue doors, boats moored in the small harbor below. Gentle forward motion with slight descent. Late-afternoon golden light, crystal-clear water. Luxury travel film style. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 2. Rice terraces at sunrise

```
Wide shot over layered green rice terraces in a mountain valley at sunrise, low mist drifting between the levels, a farmer in a conical hat walking along a narrow ridge. Very slow lateral drone slide. Soft pink and gold sunrise light. Serene nature documentary look. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 3. Overwater villa

```
Slow push-in from the turquoise lagoon toward a wooden overwater villa with a private deck and a hammock; a woman in a white linen dress steps onto the deck and looks out at the horizon. Bright tropical midday light, clear shallow water with visible sand ripples. Dreamy resort promo style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 4. Mountain hiker reveal

```
A hiker with a red backpack reaches the top of a rocky ridge and stops, the camera rising behind her to reveal a vast range of snow-capped peaks and a glacial lake far below. Smooth crane-up motion. Crisp clear morning light, deep blue sky. Epic adventure travel style. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 5. Old-town street walk

```
POV walking slowly through a narrow cobblestone old-town street in the early evening, café tables on both sides, string lights overhead, a street musician playing violin ahead. Steady gimbal movement at walking pace. Warm amber light, blue sky fading. Romantic European city travel vibe. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 6. Desert camp at night

```
Wide shot of a desert camp of canvas tents glowing warmly from inside among dark sand dunes, a campfire in the center with people sitting around it, the Milky Way bright overhead. Slow tilt up from the fire to the sky. Deep night-blue palette, warm firelight. Magical, cinematic travel look. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 7. Train window journey

```
View from inside a vintage train carriage looking out the window as forests and lakes slide past; a passenger's hand rests on a cup of tea on the small table in the foreground. Static camera, landscape moving outside. Soft overcast Nordic light, muted greens and greys. Slow-travel aesthetic. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Hotel room reveal

```
Slow dolly from the doorway of a bright boutique hotel room toward the balcony doors, which open onto a view of the sea; white curtains billow in the breeze. Clean modern interior, natural wood and linen. Soft morning sunlight. Premium hospitality promo style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. Local market colors

```
Handheld close-ups moving through a busy spice market: pyramids of bright red, yellow and orange spices, a vendor scooping saffron into a paper cone, shoppers passing. Slight push-in, natural daylight filtered through fabric awnings. Rich, vivid, authentic travel documentary feel. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 10. Northern lights

```
Wide shot of green and violet northern lights rippling across a starry sky above a snowy landscape with a small red wooden cabin, smoke rising from its chimney. Static camera, slow movement in the aurora. Deep blue night, crisp cold atmosphere. Breathtaking nature travel style. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 11. Road trip

```
A vintage turquoise convertible drives along a coastal highway carved into cliffs above the ocean, a scarf trailing from the passenger seat. Drone tracking shot following from behind and slightly above. Late-afternoon sun, glittering sea, warm saturated colors. Freedom road-trip travel style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 12. Snorkel underwater

```
Underwater shot of a snorkeler gliding over a vibrant coral reef, a school of yellow fish parting around her, sunbeams dancing through the clear turquoise water. Camera follows slowly alongside at the same depth. Bright natural underwater light, high clarity. Joyful tropical holiday style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 13. Itinerary title shot

```
Top-down shot of a wooden table with an open paper map, a passport, sunglasses, a camera and a coffee cup; a hand traces a route on the map with a finger. Locked-off overhead camera. Warm morning light, soft shadows. Travel-planning intro style, no readable text. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```
