# UGC ad prompts

User-generated-content style ads look like a real person filmed them on a phone: handheld framing, natural light, direct eye contact, imperfect but believable. They are the backbone of paid social. This pack covers creator-style shots for [AI UGC videos](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-ugc-video).

Tips for this category:

- Say "shot on a smartphone, handheld, vertical" and "natural, unpolished" — otherwise models drift toward glossy commercial lighting.
- Keep dialogue to one or two short sentences per clip. For longer scripts, use an [AI avatar](https://vivideo.ai/features/ai-avatar-generator) with an [AI voice](https://vivideo.ai/features/ai-voice-generator) instead of a raw video model.
- The person must be fictional or have consented. Disclose AI-generated testimonials; see [FTC-compliant AI testimonial videos](https://vivideo.ai/blog/ai-testimonial-videos-ftc-compliant) and the [ethics of AI video](https://vivideo.ai/blog/ethics-of-ai-video).
- Real product on screen? Start from a photo of it with [image to video](https://vivideo.ai/tools/image-to-video).

## Prompts

### 1. Selfie hook

```
Vertical selfie video shot on a smartphone at arm's length. A woman in her mid-twenties with curly hair and a grey hoodie sits in her car in a parking lot, looks straight into the lens and says: "Okay, I need to tell you about this [PRODUCT] because it actually worked." Slight handheld shake, natural daylight through the windshield, authentic and unpolished, no filters. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 2. Bathroom-mirror review

```
A man in his early thirties films himself in a bathroom mirror with his phone visible in his hand, holding up [PRODUCT] next to his face. He taps it twice and nods. Warm vanity bulb light, slightly cluttered counter, realistic skin texture, casual UGC style. Handheld, vertical framing. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 3. Desk unboxing, POV

```
First-person POV shot looking down at a desk as two hands tear open a padded mailer and pull out [PRODUCT], turning it in the light to show it off. Phone camera look, slight motion blur on fast hand movements, afternoon window light, keyboard and coffee cup at the edge of frame. Natural, unscripted feel. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 4. Walking-and-talking

```
Handheld selfie video of a young man walking down a leafy city sidewalk on a sunny day, talking to the camera with energy: "Three weeks with [PRODUCT]. Here's the honest version." Background slightly out of focus, natural bounce in the framing from walking, ambient street sound and light traffic. Authentic creator vlog style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 5. Reaction close-up

```
Tight close-up of a woman's face lit by her phone screen in a dim living room at night. She reads something, her eyebrows lift, and she breaks into a surprised laugh, covering her mouth. Shot on a front-facing phone camera, grainy low-light look, warm lamp in the background. Genuine, spontaneous reaction. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 6. Problem / solution demo

```
Vertical phone video in a small apartment kitchen. A woman holds up a stained white shirt to the camera with a frustrated look, then sprays it with [PRODUCT], rubs it with a cloth, and holds it up again clean, smiling. Handheld, natural window light, real home background. UGC demo style, single continuous take. 9:16, 10 seconds.
```

### 7. Get-ready-with-me

```
Get-ready-with-me style vertical video. A woman in her twenties sits at a vanity with a ring light reflected in her eyes, applying [PRODUCT] to her cheek with her fingertips while chatting casually to the camera. Phone on a small tripod, slight digital zoom, soft warm light, realistic skin texture with pores. Casual beauty creator aesthetic. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Gym bag check

```
Phone camera pointed down into an open gym bag on a locker-room bench. A hand pulls out a towel, headphones, then [PRODUCT], and holds it up to the lens with a thumbs-up. Fluorescent overhead light, slight greenish cast, handheld. Real, everyday fitness creator feel. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 9. Side-by-side comparison

```
Vertical phone video on a kitchen counter. Two hands hold two plain unbranded containers side by side, then pour each into a glass — the left one cloudy, the right one clear. The creator's voice says: "Same price. Not the same thing." Natural daylight, handheld, casual comparison style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 10. Couch testimonial

```
Medium shot of a man in his forties sitting on a living-room couch, phone propped on the coffee table. He speaks calmly to the camera: "I was skeptical, honestly. Two months in, I use it every day." Afternoon light from a window behind the camera, bookshelf in the background, natural pauses. Honest testimonial style, not scripted-looking. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 11. Street interview

```
Handheld vertical video on a busy pedestrian street. An off-screen interviewer holds a small microphone toward a young woman with a tote bag who laughs and answers: "Honestly? I'd buy it again." Background crowd softly blurred, natural afternoon light, ambient city noise. Casual vox-pop social style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 12. Pet-owner moment

```
Phone video on a living-room floor. A golden retriever sniffs a new [PRODUCT] toy, grabs it, and trots off proudly while its owner laughs off camera. Low handheld angle at dog height, soft afternoon light, wood floor. Warm, funny, authentic pet-owner UGC. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```
