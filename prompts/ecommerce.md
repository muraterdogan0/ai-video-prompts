# Ecommerce prompts

Motion for product pages, marketplace listings, catalog ads and shoppable social posts. Where [product ads](product-ads.md) are about one hero spot, this pack is about volume: many SKUs, consistent framing, and clips that loop cleanly on a product page. Built for [AI video for ecommerce](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-video-for-ecommerce).

Tips for this category:

- **One template, many products.** Fix the background, light and camera move, then swap only `[PRODUCT]`. That keeps a catalog looking like one store.
- Use your packshot as the start frame with [image to video](https://vivideo.ai/tools/image-to-video) so the real product, color and label survive. Cut-out photos work best; the [video background remover](https://vivideo.ai/tools/video-background-remover) helps with clips.
- Ask for "the product does not change shape, color or label" — models like to redesign things.
- Product-page videos autoplay muted: no reliance on audio, 4–6 seconds, ideally looping.

## Prompts

### 1. Catalog 360 spin

```
[PRODUCT] rotates a full 360 degrees on an invisible turntable against a seamless light grey background. Camera locked off at product height, centered. Soft even studio lighting from both sides, subtle floor shadow. The product keeps its exact shape, color and details throughout. Clean marketplace catalog style. 1:1, 6 seconds.
```

### 2. Apparel on model, walk-in

```
A model in her twenties wearing [GARMENT] walks toward the camera on a plain off-white studio set, stops, turns slightly to show the side, and puts one hand in a pocket. Full-body shot, static camera. Soft high-key fashion lighting, natural fabric movement. Clean ecommerce lookbook style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 3. Fabric close-up

```
Macro close-up as a hand slowly squeezes and releases the fabric of [GARMENT], showing its weave, stretch and drape. Camera static, shallow depth of field. Soft raking light to show texture, neutral beige background. Tactile, premium apparel detail shot. 4:5, 5 seconds.
```

### 4. Flat-lay build

```
Top-down flat-lay on a pale linen surface: items of an outfit — a folded shirt, sunglasses, a watch, sneakers, a leather wallet — slide into frame one by one from the edges and settle into a neat composition. Locked-off overhead camera. Soft daylight, gentle shadows. Stylish, organized shop-the-look aesthetic. 1:1, 8 seconds.
```

### 5. Jewelry sparkle

```
Extreme close-up of [RING / NECKLACE] resting on black velvet; a point light slowly sweeps across it, making the stones flash and sparkle. Very slow push-in, ultra-shallow depth of field. Deep black background, high-contrast luxury jewelry photography look. 4:5, 6 seconds.
```

### 6. Scale in hand

```
A hand picks up [PRODUCT] from a wooden table and holds it toward the camera at a comfortable angle, turning it slightly to show size against the palm. Medium close-up, static camera. Soft natural window light, warm neutral tones. Honest, informative product-page style. 1:1, 5 seconds.
```

### 7. Home goods in context

```
[PRODUCT] sits on a sideboard in a bright, minimalist living room; the camera slides slowly sideways, letting a plant and a lamp pass in the foreground to reveal the product in context. Warm afternoon sunlight, soft shadows, Scandinavian interior style. Aspirational but realistic home catalog look. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 8. Color variants

```
Three versions of [PRODUCT] in [RED], [SAGE GREEN] and [SAND] stand side by side on a pale pedestal. A soft spotlight moves from left to right, briefly highlighting each one. Static camera, centered composition. Even studio light, minimal pastel background. Clean variant-selector style. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 9. Sneaker drop

```
A single [SNEAKER] drops from above onto a concrete block in slow motion, bouncing slightly and kicking up a small puff of chalk dust. Low-angle close-up, static camera. Hard side light with deep shadows, urban streetwear aesthetic, grey and orange palette. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 10. Packaging to product

```
A closed [BRAND-COLOR] gift box on a white surface; the lid lifts off by itself and [PRODUCT] rises gently out of it, rotating a quarter turn before settling. Slow push-in. Soft bright lighting, subtle sparkles in the air. Premium gifting ad style, no text. 1:1, 6 seconds.
```

### 11. Size-guide demonstration

```
A woman holds up [BAG] against her shoulder in front of a plain light wall, then shifts it to carry it by the handle, showing how it sits on the body. Medium shot, static camera at chest height. Even soft lighting, neutral clothes so the product stands out. Practical, informative ecommerce style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 12. Seamless loop for product page

```
[PRODUCT] floats gently up and down a few centimeters above a soft-shadowed white surface, with a slow, constant 20-degree sway; the last frame matches the first so the clip loops seamlessly. Locked-off camera, bright even light. Clean product-page hero loop. 1:1, 5 seconds.
```

### 13. Marketplace lifestyle thumbnail

```
A smiling man in his thirties uses [PRODUCT] in a bright modern kitchen, then sets it down on the counter facing the camera. Medium shot, slight push-in to end on the product. Warm natural light, uncluttered background. Friendly, trustworthy marketplace lifestyle style. 4:5, 6 seconds.
```
