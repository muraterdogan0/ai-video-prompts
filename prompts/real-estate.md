# Real estate prompts

Listing tours, drone exteriors, twilight shots and neighborhood b-roll for [AI video for real estate](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-video-for-real-estate).

Tips for this category:

- **Use the real listing photos.** Feed each photo into [image to video](https://vivideo.ai/features/image-to-video) and prompt only the camera move and light. Text-only prompts invent rooms that do not exist, which misrepresents the property.
- Keep motion slow and architectural: dolly, slide, gentle push-in, smooth drone. Fast moves warp straight lines.
- Add "straight vertical lines, no warping, no added furniture" so the model respects the room.
- Do not let the model add features (views, pools, extensions) that the property does not have.

## Image-to-video prompts (start from a listing photo)

### 1. Living room glide

```
Slow, smooth dolly forward into the living room shown in the image, moving about one meter, eye-level height. Keep the existing furniture, windows and layout exactly as they are; straight vertical lines, no warping, nothing added. Natural daylight from the windows, sheer curtains moving very slightly. Real estate listing video style. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 2. Kitchen slide

```
Gentle lateral slide from left to right across the kitchen in the image, parallel to the island, revealing the counter and appliances. Steady gimbal motion, no change to the room's contents. Bright, clean morning light, crisp detail on surfaces. Premium property-tour look. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 3. Bedroom reveal

```
Slow pull-back from the bed toward the doorway in the image, revealing the full bedroom. Linen moves softly as if from an open window. Keep all furniture and proportions unchanged. Soft warm afternoon light, calm and inviting. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 4. Bathroom detail

```
Slow push-in toward the freestanding tub and window in the image, ending on a medium shot. Light steam rises gently from the water. Do not add objects. Soft diffused daylight, clean white and stone tones, spa-like calm. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 5. Twilight exterior

```
The house in the image at twilight: the sky shifts to deep blue with a fading orange glow on the horizon, interior lights glow warm through the windows, garden path lights switch on. Very slow push-in toward the front door. Keep the architecture exactly as shown. Luxury real estate twilight photography style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 6. Drone rise over the property

```
Starting from the front lawn of the house in the image, the camera rises smoothly like a drone and tilts down to reveal the roof, garden and street around it. Clear afternoon sky, soft shadows, natural colors. Keep the house and lot unchanged. Aerial property-tour style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

## Text-to-video prompts (neighborhood and mood b-roll)

### 7. Neighborhood morning

```
Wide shot of a quiet tree-lined residential street on a sunny spring morning, a cyclist passes and a neighbor walks a dog on the sidewalk. Slow lateral tracking shot. Soft golden morning light, fresh green leaves. Warm, welcoming community b-roll. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. City-apartment lifestyle

```
A young couple carries a box into a bright, empty modern apartment with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a city skyline, sets it down and looks around smiling. Handheld medium shot following them in. Late afternoon sunlight flooding the room. Hopeful, new-home lifestyle feel. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. Coastal property aerial

```
Smooth drone shot gliding along a coastline toward a modern white villa on a cliff above turquoise water, waves breaking softly on rocks below. Slow forward motion, slight descent. Clear midday light, high clarity, luxury travel and property aesthetic. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 10. Virtual staging mood

```
An empty, sunlit living room with oak floors and white walls; tasteful Scandinavian furniture fades in piece by piece — a sofa, a rug, a coffee table, a floor lamp — until the room is furnished. Locked-off wide shot. Soft natural light. Clean interior design visualization. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

Label virtually staged footage as such in listings.

### 11. Agent intro

```
A friendly real estate agent in her forties in a navy blazer stands on the porch of a craftsman-style house, smiles and says: "Come on in — let me show you around." She gestures to the open front door. Medium shot, slight push-in. Warm late-afternoon light. Professional, approachable. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 12. Amenities montage shot

```
Slow tracking shot along a rooftop terrace pool at sunset, loungers in a neat row, city lights beginning to twinkle in the distance, a soft breeze rippling the water. Warm golden light fading to blue. Upscale residential amenities look. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```
