# Education and training prompts

Visuals for lessons, courses, onboarding and compliance training: science and history scenes, process demonstrations, workplace scenarios and presenter-led modules. For [AI video for education](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-video-for-education) and [AI training videos](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-training-video).

Tips for this category:

- **Accuracy beats spectacle.** A video model will happily draw a wrong diagram. Use generated footage for atmosphere and analogy; put facts, labels and numbers in narration and overlays you control.
- One concept per clip. Learners follow a slow, clear motion better than a busy one.
- Training scenarios should show correct behavior clearly and, if you show mistakes, make them obvious and non-graphic.
- Most courses need several languages. Generate once, then use the [AI video translator](https://vivideo.ai/features/ai-video-translator); see [how to translate a video](https://vivideo.ai/guides/how-to-translate-a-video) and [how to add subtitles](https://vivideo.ai/guides/how-to-add-subtitles-to-video).

## Prompts

### 1. Cell biology fly-through

```
Smooth camera glide through the inside of a stylized animal cell: translucent membrane, glowing nucleus in the center, small bean-shaped mitochondria drifting past, a web of thin filaments. Slow forward motion toward the nucleus. Soft bioluminescent blues and greens, clean scientific 3D illustration style, no labels. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 2. Solar system scale

```
Wide space shot of the Sun glowing on the left; the camera pulls back steadily as the inner planets come into view as small spheres on a faint orbital line. Deep black space with a dense star field. Clean educational astronomy visualization, soft glow on the Sun, no text. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 3. Water cycle diorama

```
Miniature diorama of a valley: sunlight warms a lake, faint vapor rises into a cloud above the mountains, the cloud darkens and rain falls on the slopes, a stream runs back to the lake. Slow orbit around the diorama. Bright, soft studio lighting, tactile clay-and-felt style. Friendly primary-school science look. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 4. Historical scene recreation

```
A bustling medieval market square in the early morning: merchants set up wooden stalls, a cart loaded with barrels rolls over cobblestones, smoke rises from a bakery chimney. Slow crane shot descending from rooftop level into the square. Soft misty sunlight, earthy colors, documentary realism. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 5. Math visualization

```
A clean geometric animation on a dark background: a square splits into two right triangles, the triangles rotate and slide to form a larger square with a smaller tilted square in the middle, all edges glowing softly in gold. Static camera, top-down. Minimal, precise, elegant math-explainer style, no numbers. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 6. Lab safety demonstration

```
A lab technician in a white coat, safety goggles and nitrile gloves carefully pours a clear liquid from a beaker into a flask inside a fume hood, keeping the beaker low and steady. Medium shot, static camera at bench height. Bright even laboratory lighting, clean organized bench. Calm, instructional training style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 7. Workplace onboarding welcome

```
A friendly team lead in her thirties walks a new employee through a bright open-plan office, pointing out a kitchen area and smiling as colleagues wave. Tracking shot walking alongside them. Soft daylight, modern office, diverse team. Warm, welcoming corporate onboarding style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Customer-service scenario

```
At a hotel reception desk, a receptionist listens attentively to a guest, nods, types briefly, and hands over a key card with a smile. Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the guest, then a slow push toward the receptionist. Warm lobby lighting, professional and calm. Soft-skills training scenario. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. Manual-handling technique

```
A warehouse worker in a high-visibility vest demonstrates lifting a cardboard box correctly: feet apart, knees bent, back straight, box held close to the body, standing up slowly. Side-on full-body shot, static camera. Bright, even warehouse lighting. Clear health-and-safety training style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 10. Language-learning scene

```
A small café counter in Lisbon: a customer orders, the barista smiles and slides over a small coffee and a custard tart on a saucer. Medium two-shot, gentle handheld. Warm morning light, tiled walls, authentic everyday atmosphere. Clear, slow gestures suitable for a language lesson. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 11. Classroom attention hook

```
A science teacher in a bright classroom drops a dry-ice pellet into a beaker of warm water; thick white fog spills over the rim and across the desk while students in the front row lean forward. Medium shot, slow push-in on the beaker. Daylight from tall windows. Engaging, real classroom feel. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 12. Healthcare patient education

```
A calm nurse in light blue scrubs sits beside a patient in a bright clinic room and shows her how to use an inhaler, demonstrating slowly with a training device. Medium two-shot, static camera. Soft natural light, reassuring and respectful tone. Patient-education video style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 13. Course trailer opener

```
Slow push-in through a sunlit university library: long wooden tables, a student turns a page in an old book, dust motes float in shafts of light from tall arched windows. Warm golden tones, shallow depth of field, cinematic documentary look. Inspiring online-course trailer opening. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```
