# Product ad prompts

Short, high-polish shots for [AI ad videos](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-ad-video): hero reveals, macro texture, liquid and particle effects, and lifestyle moments. Replace anything in `[BRACKETS]` with your product.

Tips for this category:

- For a real product, start from a clean product photo and use [image to video](https://vivideo.ai/features/image-to-video). Text-only prompts will invent the packaging, label and logo.
- Keep the camera move slow and the background simple; the product should stay sharp and readable the whole time.
- Ask for "no text, no logo" and add your own copy afterwards with [add text to video](https://vivideo.ai/tools/add-text-to-video). Models still misspell on-screen words.
- Cut 3–5 of these shots together for a 15–30 second spot. Keep colors consistent with a [brand kit](https://vivideo.ai/features/brand-kit).

## Prompts

### 1. Turntable hero

```
A matte black [PRODUCT] stands on a slowly rotating white plinth in an infinite white studio. The camera holds a locked-off medium close-up at product height while the plinth turns 90 degrees. Large softbox key light from the left, thin rim light from behind outlining the edges, soft shadow under the base. Clean commercial product photography look, crisp detail, no text, no logo. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 2. Macro texture reveal

```
Extreme macro shot gliding slowly across the surface of [PRODUCT], revealing [brushed aluminium / stitched leather / frosted glass] texture in razor-sharp detail. Shallow depth of field, focus rolls from foreground to background as the camera slides sideways. Single hard light from a low angle raking across the surface to bring out texture, dark background. Premium luxury ad aesthetic. 16:9, 5 seconds.
```

### 3. Liquid splash

```
A chilled can of [DRINK] drops into a pool of crystal-clear water in slow motion, sending a crown-shaped splash and droplets into the air around it. Camera static at eye level, high-speed capture look at 1000 fps. Backlit with bright white light so every droplet glows, pale blue gradient background. Condensation beads on the can. Hyper-real beverage commercial style, no text. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 4. Floating ingredients

```
[PRODUCT] bottle floats in the center of frame while its key ingredients — sliced lemon, mint leaves, ice cubes and a few water droplets — drift slowly around it in zero gravity. Slow 180-degree orbit around the bottle. Soft top light with a subtle green gradient background. Fresh, clean, airy commercial look, sharp product label area kept blank. 1:1, 8 seconds.
```

### 5. Unboxing from above

```
Top-down overhead shot of hands opening a minimalist kraft-paper box on a light oak table. The lid lifts away to reveal [PRODUCT] nested in tissue paper; the hands fold back the tissue slowly. Soft window daylight from the top of frame, gentle shadows. Calm, tactile, premium direct-to-consumer brand aesthetic. Camera locked off. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 6. Launch teaser silhouette

```
[PRODUCT] sits in near-total darkness. A thin line of light sweeps slowly from left to right across it, revealing only its silhouette and one edge at a time. Camera performs a very slow push-in. Haze in the air catches the beam. Moody, high-contrast tech launch teaser, deep blacks, cool white light. No text. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 7. Lifestyle in use

```
A woman in her thirties uses [PRODUCT] at a sunlit kitchen counter in the morning, glancing up and smiling naturally at someone off camera. Medium shot, slow handheld drift to the right, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field. Warm golden sunlight through the window, light steam from a coffee mug. Natural, authentic lifestyle commercial look, not posed. 4:5, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Material morph

```
Close-up of [PRODUCT] on a stone pedestal. The surface around it transforms in a smooth continuous morph from raw marble, to polished wood, to brushed brass, while the product stays perfectly still and sharp. Locked-off camera, soft diffused key light, warm neutral palette. Elegant, design-magazine aesthetic. 1:1, 6 seconds.
```

### 9. Speed and motion (sport product)

```
Low tracking shot just above wet asphalt following a runner's feet wearing [SHOE] at night, splashing through a shallow puddle in slow motion. Camera moves at the runner's speed, 24mm wide lens. Neon city reflections in the water, cyan and magenta rim light, spray of droplets frozen in the air. Energetic sports commercial look. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 10. Exploded view

```
[PRODUCT] separates slowly into its component parts, each piece floating outward along clean axes to form an exploded view, then holds. Camera makes a slow 45-degree orbit during the separation. Neutral grey studio, soft even lighting, precise engineering-visualization style, photoreal materials, no labels, no text. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 11. Skincare texture swatch

```
Macro shot of a fingertip drawing a slow swirl through a dollop of pale pink cream on a glass surface, the cream folding with a glossy, thick texture. Camera static, very shallow depth of field. Soft diffused daylight from the side, blush pink background. Clean beauty commercial aesthetic, skin texture natural and realistic. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 12. Before and after split

```
Split-screen composition, vertical divide down the center. Left side: a cluttered, dim desk with tangled cables. Right side: the same desk organized with [PRODUCT], bright and tidy. A slow push-in on both halves at the same time. Left lit with cold flat light, right lit with warm soft daylight. Realistic, relatable home-office ad style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 13. Seasonal hero

```
[PRODUCT] sits on a snow-dusted wooden log in a quiet pine forest at blue hour. Fine snowflakes fall slowly in the foreground, out of focus. Slow push-in from a medium shot to a close-up. Warm string-light bokeh in the background, cool blue ambient light, warm accent on the product. Cozy holiday campaign aesthetic, no text. 4:5, 8 seconds.
```
