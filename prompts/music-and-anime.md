# Music video and anime prompts

Performance shots, abstract visuals for tracks, looping visualizers and anime-style scenes. For [AI music videos](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-music-video) and [AI anime videos](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-anime-video).

Tips for this category:

- **Cut to the beat, not inside the clip.** Generate 4–8 second shots and edit them on the beat. Note the track's tempo and plan shot lengths in bars.
- Pick one visual world per song (palette, era, medium) and repeat its style line in every prompt.
- For anime, name the look precisely: "cel-shaded 2D anime", "hand-painted backgrounds", "limited animation", "soft bloom". Do not reference specific studios, films or characters — describe the look instead.
- Add the track afterwards with [add music to video](https://vivideo.ai/tools/add-music-to-video) and mute generated audio with [mute video](https://vivideo.ai/tools/mute-video) if the model adds its own.

## Music video prompts

### 1. Neon performance

```
A singer with silver hair in a black leather jacket performs into a vintage microphone on a small stage, eyes closed, swaying. Slow 180-degree orbit around her. Magenta and cyan neon backlight, haze, lens flares, deep shadows. Stylish music-video cinematography. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 2. Lo-fi bedroom loop

```
A young person with headphones sits at a desk by a rainy window at night, writing in a notebook, a cat asleep beside the lamp. Static medium shot, rain streaks on the glass, city lights blurred outside. Warm desk lamp, soft muted colors, cozy lo-fi illustration style; the last frame matches the first for a seamless loop. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 3. Dancer in the desert

```
A contemporary dancer in a flowing red dress spins slowly on white sand dunes, the fabric trailing in the wind. Wide shot, camera circling at a distance. Golden-hour backlight, long shadows, sand blowing off the dune crest. Dreamy, cinematic music-video style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 4. Abstract visualizer

```
Liquid chrome blobs merge and split in slow, rhythmic pulses against a deep black void, their surfaces reflecting moving bands of violet and orange light. Camera slowly drifts closer. Hypnotic, high-gloss 3D abstract visual for a music visualizer, seamless loop. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 5. Band on a rooftop

```
A four-piece indie band plays on a city rooftop at sunset — drums, bass, guitar, and a singer — the skyline glowing behind them. Handheld camera moves between band members with energy. Warm sunset backlight, lens flares, slight film grain, 16mm documentary music-video look. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 6. Retro VHS memory

```
A group of friends laugh and run along a beach boardwalk at dusk, one of them filming on an old camcorder. Handheld footage with VHS look: soft focus, color bleeding, scan lines, slightly washed-out colors, date-stamp area left blank. Nostalgic 1990s home-video feel. 4:3, 8 seconds.
```

### 7. Album-art come alive

```
A surreal still-life — a giant moon resting in a field of tall blue flowers under a pink sky — comes slowly to life: the flowers sway, clouds drift, the moon's surface glows faintly. Very slow push-in. Soft painterly surrealism, pastel palette. Album-cover animation, seamless loop. 1:1, 8 seconds.
```

## Anime and stylized prompts

### 8. Anime rooftop at sunset

```
Cel-shaded 2D anime style: a high-school girl with a short ponytail and loose tie stands at a rooftop railing at sunset, her hair and skirt moving in the wind, cherry petals drifting past. Slow pan from the city skyline to her profile. Hand-painted background, warm orange sky, soft bloom, gentle limited-animation feel. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. Anime action moment

```
2D anime action shot: a young swordsman in a dark blue coat dashes forward across a moonlit bamboo forest, his blade leaving a bright white arc, leaves exploding outward around him. Fast tracking shot, dynamic speed lines, dramatic low angle. High-contrast night palette with cyan highlights. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 10. Slice-of-life train

```
Anime-style interior of a quiet countryside train in summer: a boy dozes against the window as fields and power lines pass outside, sunlight flickering across his face. Static medium shot. Hand-painted backgrounds, soft pastel colors, gentle nostalgic mood, cicada ambience. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 11. Chibi mascot

```
Cute chibi-style character with a big head and tiny body, wearing a yellow raincoat, hops through puddles on a rainy street, each splash sending sparkles. Static side view, character moves across the frame. Bright flat colors, thick clean outlines, playful 2D animation. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 12. Stylized 3D music character

```
A stylized 3D character — a small robot with a speaker for a face — bobs its head to a beat in a colorful bedroom studio full of glowing synths, then points at the camera. Medium shot, slight push-in. Soft toy-like materials, pastel purple and teal lighting. Playful music-channel mascot style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 13. Ink-wash landscape

```
Traditional East Asian ink-wash painting comes to life: misty mountains, a lone fisherman on a small boat drifting on a still river, ink spreading softly as the mist moves. Slow horizontal pan. Monochrome black ink on textured rice paper with a single red accent. Calm, meditative. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```
