# Explainer video prompts

Explainers turn an idea into something you can see: a process, a product, a concept. The best AI explainers combine a narrated script with visual metaphors and clean motion graphics. This pack covers shots for [AI explainer videos](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-explainer-video), SaaS walkthroughs and faceless channels.

Tips for this category:

- Write the script first, then prompt one shot per sentence or idea. [How to write a video script](https://vivideo.ai/guides/how-to-write-a-video-script) covers structure; the [script timer](https://vivideo.ai/tools/script-timer) tells you how long the narration will run.
- Video models are weak at readable text and real UI. Ask for "abstract interface", "blank cards" or "no readable text", and overlay your real screenshots and labels in editing.
- A consistent visual language (same palette, same style words in every prompt) matters more than any single shot.
- For a full narrated explainer from a brief, Vivideo's [agentic chat](https://vivideo.ai/features/agentic-chat) writes and assembles the whole thing.

## Prompts

### 1. Concept metaphor: data flow

```
Clean 3D motion-graphics scene: glowing blue particles stream along curved transparent tubes from many small cubes on the left into one large sphere on the right, which pulses softly as it fills. Slow sideways camera dolly. Dark navy background, soft cyan glow, minimal and modern tech explainer style, no text. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 2. Isometric workflow

```
Isometric 3D illustration of a tiny office: a document slides out of a printer, travels along a conveyor belt through three stations — a magnifying glass, a gear, and a green checkmark stamp — and drops into a box. Camera static isometric view. Soft pastel palette, smooth toy-like materials, gentle shadows. Friendly SaaS explainer style, no readable text. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 3. Abstract dashboard

```
A floating, semi-transparent dashboard interface hovers in a dark space: abstract bar charts grow upward, a line graph draws itself from left to right, and circular gauges fill to 80%. Slow orbit around the panels. Glassmorphism style, purple and teal accents, soft bloom. No readable text or numbers. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 4. Whiteboard style

```
Hand-drawn whiteboard animation: a black marker sketches a simple light bulb, then lines branch out from it to a rocket, a chart, and a person icon, each drawn in a few quick strokes. Camera static on a white board, slight paper texture, the drawing hand partly visible. Clear, educational doodle style. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 5. Problem shot (relatable)

```
A tired man in his thirties at a home desk at night, surrounded by sticky notes and three open laptops, rubs his eyes as notifications pop up as glowing abstract bubbles around his head. Slow push-in from medium to close-up. Cold monitor light on his face, dark room, warm lamp in the background. Cinematic, relatable explainer opening. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 6. Solution shot (relief)

```
The same home office in morning daylight, now tidy: one laptop open, a plant on the desk, a calm woman in her thirties leans back, sips coffee and smiles at the screen. Slow pull-back to a wide shot. Bright soft window light, warm neutral palette. Clean, optimistic explainer style. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 7. Paper cut-out process

```
Layered paper cut-out animation: a small paper seed falls into soil, sprouts, grows into a tree, and the tree's leaves turn into coins that drift upward. Camera static, layers moving with subtle parallax. Warm craft-paper textures, earthy greens and golds, soft drop shadows. Charming financial explainer style. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 8. Exploded system diagram

```
A sleek smartphone hovers in the center of frame, then its layers separate vertically — glass, display, circuit board, battery — with thin glowing lines connecting each layer to empty label circles on the side. Slow 30-degree orbit. Dark grey studio, crisp technical lighting, precise 3D visualization style, labels left blank. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 9. Faceless narration b-roll: history

```
Slow aerial drone shot drifting over the ruins of an ancient stone amphitheater at sunrise, long shadows across the terraces, mist in the valley beyond. Gentle forward motion, wide lens. Warm golden light, documentary realism, subtle film grain. Atmospheric b-roll for a narrated history video. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 10. Faceless narration b-roll: money

```
Macro shot of coins being stacked one by one into rising columns on a dark wooden table, each column slightly taller than the last, the camera slowly tracking sideways along them. Single warm key light from the right, deep shadows, shallow depth of field. Moody finance documentary look. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 11. Character-guide explainer

```
A friendly round robot with a single glowing blue eye stands next to a large floating cube and gestures toward it with one hand, then the cube unfolds into a flat map. Medium shot, slight camera push-in. Soft studio lighting, bright 3D animated style, clean white and orange palette. Approachable explainer mascot style. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 12. Timeline sweep

```
A long horizontal timeline made of glowing dots on a dark surface; the camera tracks slowly from left to right along it as small 3D icons rise from each dot — a steam engine, a light bulb, a radio, a computer, a rocket. Elegant museum-exhibit style lighting, warm and cool contrast, no text. 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

### 13. Split comparison

```
Split-screen 3D scene divided down the middle: left side shows a slow, cluttered conveyor with packages piling up in red light; right side shows a fast, orderly conveyor with packages flowing smoothly in green light. Static camera, symmetrical composition. Clean illustrative 3D style, no text. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```
