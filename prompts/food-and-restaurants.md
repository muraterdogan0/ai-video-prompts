# Food and restaurant prompts

Appetite appeal in motion: steam, sizzle, pours, cheese pulls, plating, and the room around the food. For [AI video for restaurants](https://vivideo.ai/solutions/ai-video-for-restaurants), cafés, bakeries, food brands and recipe channels.

Tips for this category:

- **Motion sells food.** Every prompt should have something moving: steam rising, sauce dripping, a knife cutting, bubbles in a glass.
- Backlight or side-light food; front light flattens it. "Backlit steam" is the single most useful phrase in this pack.
- For your real dishes, animate your own photos with [image to video](https://vivideo.ai/features/image-to-video) so customers see what they will actually get.
- Keep hands clean and plausible, and avoid showing the same dish with different garnishes across a campaign.

## Prompts

### 1. Ramen steam

```
Close-up of a steaming bowl of ramen with a soft-boiled egg, sliced chashu, scallions and nori on a dark wooden counter; chopsticks lift a tangle of noodles slowly toward the camera. Backlit so the steam glows. Slow push-in, shallow depth of field. Warm, moody Japanese restaurant atmosphere. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

### 2. Burger build

```
A gourmet burger assembles itself in slow motion from the bottom up on a slate board: brioche bun, juicy patty with melted cheddar, lettuce, tomato, pickles, top bun landing with a slight bounce, sesame seeds scattering. Side-on static camera. Dark background, warm side light, glossy textures. Appetizing fast-casual ad style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 3. Pizza cheese pull

```
A hand lifts a slice from a wood-fired margherita pizza, long strands of melted mozzarella stretching from the pie. Close-up, camera slowly rising with the slice. Warm light from the glowing oven behind, flecks of basil, charred crust detail. Authentic pizzeria feel. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

### 4. Coffee pour

```
Overhead macro shot of a barista pouring steamed milk into an espresso cup, forming a rosetta latte-art pattern. Locked-off camera directly above. Soft window light, ceramic cup on a light wood counter. Calm specialty-coffee aesthetic, crisp detail on the crema. 1:1, 6 seconds.
```

### 5. Sizzling pan

```
Close-up of a cast-iron pan as thick slices of steak hit hot oil and butter, sizzling and spitting; a sprig of rosemary and crushed garlic are tossed in. Low side angle at pan height, slight handheld movement. Backlit smoke, warm orange flame glow below. Intense, appetizing chef's-kitchen style, loud sizzle. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 6. Bakery morning

```
A baker in a linen apron pulls a tray of golden croissants out of a deck oven at dawn, steam rising, flakes of pastry catching the light. Medium shot, slow push-in toward the tray. Warm tungsten and early blue daylight mix, flour dust in the air. Artisan bakery documentary look. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 7. Cocktail build

```
A bartender pours a deep amber cocktail over a large clear ice cube in a crystal glass, then expresses an orange peel over the top, a fine mist catching the light. Medium close-up, static camera at bar height. Dark moody bar, warm backlight, glossy reflections on the bar top. Upscale cocktail-lounge style. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 8. Chocolate drizzle

```
Slow-motion macro of glossy melted dark chocolate drizzling over a stack of fluffy pancakes topped with fresh raspberries, the chocolate pooling on the plate. Static camera at a 30-degree angle. Soft warm side light, cream background. Indulgent dessert ad style. 4:5, 6 seconds.
```

### 9. Fresh salad toss

```
Top-down shot of a large ceramic bowl as hands toss a bright salad of leaves, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta and olives; dressing glistens and a few leaves lift into the air. Locked-off overhead camera. Bright natural daylight, fresh green and red palette. Healthy, vibrant restaurant menu style. 1:1, 6 seconds.
```

### 10. Recipe step: knife work

```
Close-up on a wooden cutting board as a chef finely dices a red onion with fast, confident knife strokes, pushing the pieces aside with the blade. Static camera at a 45-degree angle. Bright, even kitchen light, clean counter. Clear recipe-tutorial style. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

### 11. Restaurant ambience

```
Wide shot of a busy neighborhood bistro at dinner time: candles on tables, couples talking and laughing, a waiter carrying two plates past the camera. Slow dolly along the room. Warm amber lighting, soft bokeh from string lights, gentle crowd murmur and clinking glasses. Inviting, lively atmosphere. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

### 12. Street food stall

```
Night market street-food stall: a cook flips skewers over glowing charcoal, smoke and sparks rising, customers waiting in the background. Handheld medium shot, slight push-in. Warm firelight against cool blue night, lantern bokeh. Vibrant travel-food documentary feel. 9:16, 8 seconds.
```

### 13. Delivery-ready hero

```
A takeaway box opens on a clean counter to reveal a neatly packed poke bowl — salmon, avocado, edamame, rice, sesame seeds — and chopsticks are placed beside it. Slow push-in from above at 45 degrees. Bright soft daylight, minimalist background. Fresh, appetizing delivery-app style. 1:1, 6 seconds.
```
